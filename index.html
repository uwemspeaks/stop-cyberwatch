02 / RESPONSIBLE REPORTING

Action Desk

Share information about a website, application, channel or service you suspect is distributing copyrighted content without authorisation.

  1. Describe what you observed.
  2. Provide specific public URLs and evidence notes.
  3. Submit for human assessment.
Important

A submission is an allegation for assessment, not a confirmed finding. Do not access private systems, confront operators or attempt to disrupt a service.
